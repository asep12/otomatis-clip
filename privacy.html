Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Otomatis Clip is a personal desktop application for creating short vertical videos. This policy explains what data the app accesses, where it is stored, and how you can delete it. The app runs locally on your own computer; we do not operate a server that stores your videos or TikTok account data.

1. Data we access from TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, the app uses TikTok Login Kit with the user.info.basic scope. This gives the app:

  • your TikTok open_id (an app-specific account identifier);
  • your display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok.

For uploads, the app uses the Content Posting API with the video.upload scope. Only the video file you explicitly select is sent to TikTok.

2. How we use the data

  • To authenticate you with TikTok and display the connected account.
  • To upload a video you selected to your own TikTok inbox.
  • To refresh the access token so the connection keeps working.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to these functions.

3. Where data is stored

OAuth tokens, the TikTok open ID, app settings, and rendered videos are stored on your local computer. Video files are sent directly from your computer to TikTok's upload servers. We do not upload your videos to any server controlled by us.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Data is sent to TikTok only as part of the features you request (login and upload). TikTok's own privacy policy applies to data processed by TikTok.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens remain on your computer until you disconnect the TikTok account in the app or delete the app's local data. Disconnecting removes the stored tokens. You can also revoke the app's access at any time from your TikTok account settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).

6. Security

The app stores OAuth credentials locally and does not expose your TikTok password. The client secret used for OAuth is kept in the app's local configuration and is never sent to your browser or shared with third parties.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and is intended for personal creator use.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact beritaotomatis@gmail.com.
