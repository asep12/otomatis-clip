Desktop app · Local processing

Turn long videos into short vertical clips.

Otomatis Clip analyzes a long video, finds the strongest moments, renders them as 9:16 clips with animated subtitles, and sends the finished files to your own TikTok inbox for review.

See how it works Privacy Policy Terms of Service
STEP 01

Analyze

Paste a YouTube, Google Drive, or local video. The app extracts the transcript and scores the most engaging moments.

STEP 02

Render

Clips are cut on sentence boundaries, cropped to 9:16, and rendered with subtitles and optional music.

STEP 03

Send to TikTok

Choose a clip and upload it to your TikTok inbox. You review and publish it yourself in the TikTok app.

TikTok integration

The app uses TikTok Login Kit with the user.info.basic scope to show which TikTok account is connected. Uploads use the TikTok Content Posting API with the video.upload scope, which sends a video the user explicitly selected to that creator's own TikTok inbox.

Otomatis Clip does not publish automatically and does not use the Direct Post scope. The creator reviews the clip and completes the post inside the TikTok app.
